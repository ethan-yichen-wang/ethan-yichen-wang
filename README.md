# Yichen Wang

You can also call me Ethan. I am an M.Sc. student in Financial Technology at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, with an undergraduate background in Electronic Information Engineering and Finance from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China.

My research interests lie in market microstructure and learning-based decision making in finance. I am particularly interested in how information and market rules shape prices and decisions under uncertainty.

## Selected work

**Put–Call Ratios and Commodity Futures Returns**

A research project examining whether option put–call ratios provide information about next-day commodity futures returns.

- Official data from five Chinese commodity exchanges; a core panel of 43,079 commodity-days across 61 products, spanning 2019–2025.
- A frozen analysis plan, panel regressions, and a fixed-model historical holdout evaluation for 2025, with data-exposure limitations explicitly documented.
- Independently checked computations. No clear next-day predictive evidence in the prespecified linear specifications.

The code and report are being prepared for public release.
